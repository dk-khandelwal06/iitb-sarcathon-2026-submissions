## 📈 01 — Portfolio Playoff 2026

> **A tactical investment strategy built for a short competition window.**

The Portfolio Playoff submission focuses on constructing a disciplined equity portfolio using **₹10,00,000 of virtual capital** across **10 stocks** from the official competition universe.

### 🎯 What We Built

Our approach combines:

- 📊 Market regime analysis
- 🔎 Quantitative screening
- ⚡ Near-term catalysts
- 🧠 Investment theses
- ⚖️ Tactical position sizing
- 🛡️ Risk architecture
- 📉 Bear / Base / Bull scenarios
- 🎯 Target-price methodology

A key part of our framework is:

### **PCAR — Probability-Weighted Catalyst Alpha & Risk**

The portfolio was designed specifically around the competition's short tracking horizon rather than a traditional long-term buy-and-hold strategy.

**Virtual Capital:** ₹10,00,000  
**Stocks:** 10  
**Allocation:** 100%

## 🚪 02 — Bizarreo 2026

> ### **DOORCEPS**
> **Turn Every Doorway Into a Rep.**

What if your house was already a gym — and you simply weren't using it that way?

DOORCEPS is a creative product concept that transforms an ordinary doorway interaction into a small opportunity for physical activity.

### 💡 The Insight

> **"You don't have a motivation problem.  
> You have a time problem."**

Instead of asking people to build another habit, DOORCEPS upgrades something they already do:

**Opening a door.**

### ⚙️ The Concept

A handle is intentionally positioned near the **hinge side** of the door.

This changes the leverage required to open the door and creates additional resistance.

**Core features:**

- 🔩 Clip-on installation
- 🚫 No drilling
- ✋ Ergonomic grip
- 🎚️ Adjustable resistance
- 🚪 Hinge-side positioning
- 🔄 Removable & adjustable
- ⏱️ Zero additional workout time

### 🎯 Target Users

- 💻 Work-from-home professionals
- 🎓 Students
- 🏢 Office & workplace wellness users

## 🌾 03 — Agentic AI Hackathon 2026

> ### **FasalRakshak — फसल रक्षक**
> **Deadline-Aware PMFBY Loss Intimation Agent**

FasalRakshak is an **agentic AI concept** designed to help small and marginal farmers report crop losses through **WhatsApp and voice in Hindi/Hinglish**.

The focus is not simply answering questions.

The system is designed to **understand → plan → act → verify → recover**.

### 🚨 The Problem

Farmers may face difficulty reporting crop damage within the applicable **72-hour loss-intimation window** because of:

- Complex portals
- English-heavy forms
- Unstructured information
- Communication failures
- Missing or mismatched details
- Limited digital accessibility

### 🤖 Agentic Workflow

```text
Report
   ↓
Ingest
   ↓
Clarify
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Plan
   ↓
Check
   ↓
Confirm
   ↓
Act
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Recover
   ↓
Status
   ↓
Remember
```

### 🏗️ Proposed Architecture

```text
        👨‍🌾 Farmer
       WhatsApp / Voice
              │
              ▼
     ┌─────────────────┐
     │   Agent Core    │
     │ LLM + Planning  │
     │ + State         │
     └─────────────────┘
        ↙      ↓      ↘
     OCR      PMFBY    IMD
   /Vision     APIs   Weather
        ↘      ↓      ↙
              │
              ▼
       ✅ Verified Outcome
```

### 🛡️ Reliability by Design

FasalRakshak includes:

- Explicit consent before consequential actions
- No fabricated success
- Human verification for critical information
- Confidence thresholds
- Failure recovery
- Fallback pathways
- Minimal PII retention
- State persistence

Where government integrations are not publicly accessible, the submission explicitly identifies components as MOCK or SIMULATED rather than claiming live integration.
