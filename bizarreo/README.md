## 🚪 Concept

### DOORCEPS
**Turn Every Doorway Into a Rep.**

DOORCEPS is a creative product concept that transforms an ordinary doorway into an opportunity for incidental physical activity.

The idea challenges conventional fitness solutions by turning an existing everyday movement — opening a door — into a small moment of resistance-based exercise, without requiring a separate workout schedule, gym visit, app, or new habit.

## 💡 The Insight

**You don't have a motivation problem.  
You have a time problem.**

Instead of asking people to create another habit, DOORCEPS upgrades a movement they already perform every day.

### The Product

DOORCEPS uses a hinge-side door-handle placement to increase the force required to open the door, turning a routine doorway interaction into a small physical effort.

Key characteristics:

- Clip-on mounting — no drilling
- Ergonomic grip surface
- Adjustable resistance dial
- Hinge-side positioning
- Removable and adjustable
- No permanent modification

## 🎯 Target Users

DOORCEPS is designed around people who struggle to find time for conventional exercise, including:

- Work-from-home professionals
- Students
- Office and workplace wellness users

The concept focuses on creating **incidental activity** rather than demanding another scheduled workout.
