## 📈 Submission

This submission presents our tactical investment strategy and portfolio developed for the **Portfolio Playoff 2026**.

Our Phase 1 portfolio is built around **₹10,00,000 in virtual capital** allocated across **10 selected stocks** from the official 30-stock universe.

### Key Components

- Executive investment strategy
- Competition framework and official constraints
- Approved stock universe
- Short-term investment mandate
- Market regime analysis
- **PCAR — Probability-Weighted Catalyst Alpha & Risk** framework
- Quantitative screening methodology
- Final 10-stock portfolio
- Individual stock investment theses
- Sector exposure and allocation
- Risk architecture
- Bear, Base and Bull case scenario analysis
- Target price methodology
- Timeline and data-integrity assumptions
- Compliance audit
- Research & references
- Portfolio mathematics and constraint verification

## 🎯 Investment Approach

Our strategy is designed specifically for the competition's short tracking window rather than a traditional long-term buy-and-hold approach.

The portfolio emphasizes:

- Near-term catalysts
- Probability-weighted opportunities
- Downside protection
- Tactical position sizing
- Sector diversification
- Risk discipline

The portfolio consists of **10 equities with 100% of the ₹10,00,000 virtual capital allocated**.
